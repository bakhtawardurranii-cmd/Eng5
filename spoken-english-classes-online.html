Why this program

What Makes This Program Different?

Most online spoken English classes in Pakistan are either pre-recorded content dressed up as "classes," or live sessions so large you barely speak. Ours are live, small, and built so every student talks in every session — not just listens.

Questions

What Should You Know Before You Start?

Is this really live, not recorded videos?

Yes — every session is live over video call with an instructor and fellow students, not pre-recorded content.

How many people are in each class?

Groups are kept small, typically 4 to 6 students, so everyone gets meaningful speaking time.

What if I miss a class?

Let us know in advance where possible; we can usually accommodate a makeup session or provide a recap.

Do I need any equipment beyond a laptop or phone?

No — just a stable internet connection and a device with a camera and microphone.

What platform are classes held on?

Sessions run over a standard video call platform — you'll get a link before each class, no special software required.

Can I try one class before committing?

Yes, new students can join a trial class to see the format and teaching style before enrolling.
