What Does Each IELTS Band Actually Mean?

A quick overview — for the full breakdown of every skill within each band, see our IELTS Band Descriptors guide.

BandLevelWhat it means
9Expert UserFull operational command of English, appropriate, accurate, and fluent.
8Very Good UserFully operational command, with only occasional unsystematic errors.
7Good UserOperational command, though with occasional inaccuracies in unfamiliar situations.
6Competent UserGenerally effective command despite some inaccuracies and misunderstandings.
5Modest UserPartial command, coping with overall meaning in most situations.
4Limited UserBasic competence limited to familiar situations.
Questions

What Should You Know Before You Book?

Is the free IELTS mock test really free?

Yes. All four modules — Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking — are assessed at no cost, with no obligation to enroll in a course afterward.

Is this an official IELTS score?

No. This is an independent mock assessment scored by our instructors using official IELTS band descriptors. It isn't administered by IDP or the British Council and won't appear on your official IELTS record — it's designed to closely estimate the band you'd receive on test day.

How long does the full mock test take?

Listening, Reading, and Writing take about 2 hours 40 minutes in total, matching real IELTS timing. The Speaking interview is scheduled separately and takes 11 to 14 minutes.

How soon do I get my band score and feedback?

You'll receive your estimated band score and a written feedback report within 3 to 5 working days after completing all four modules.

Can I take the mock test for General Training instead of Academic?

Yes. Let us know which version you're preparing for when you book, and we'll assess you against the correct module.
