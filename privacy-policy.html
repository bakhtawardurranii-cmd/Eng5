Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains what information English Learning Studio collects, how it's used, and how it's protected.

1. Who We Are

English Learning Studio is an English language and IELTS coaching studio based at 631B, 6th Floor, Mashriq Center, Hassan Square, Gulshan-e-Iqbal Block 14, Karachi 74550, Pakistan. This policy applies to information collected through englishlearningstudio.online and through direct contact with the studio (phone, WhatsApp, or email).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you fill out a form on this site — for example, to book a free assessment, book the free IELTS mock test, or send us a message. This typically includes your name, phone number, email address, and any details you choose to share about your goals or questions. We do not knowingly collect payment card details, national identity numbers, or other sensitive personal information through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiries, schedule assessments and sessions, send you information about programs you've asked about, and improve the free tools and resources on this site. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. How Forms Are Processed

Forms on this site (the Contact form and the Free IELTS Mock Test booking form) are processed using Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service that forwards your submission to our email inbox. Web3Forms does not use your information for its own marketing and acts only as a delivery mechanism on our behalf. We recommend reviewing Web3Forms' own privacy policy for details on how they handle data in transit.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This site may use cookies or similar technologies, along with tools such as Google Analytics and Google Search Console, to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it over time. These tools may collect information such as your browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Data Retention

We retain the information you submit through our forms for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, and maintain accurate business records, after which it may be deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, or delete your information from our records, by emailing hello@englishlearningstudio.online. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's Privacy

Our programs are designed for adults. This site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at hello@englishlearningstudio.online or +92 370 2491776. See also our Terms of Service.
