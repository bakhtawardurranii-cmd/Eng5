Why this program

What Makes This Program Different?

Beginners often get placed in mixed-level groups where more advanced students dominate the conversation. This program keeps true beginners together, so the pace and the room both match where you actually are.

Questions

What Should You Know Before You Start?

I've never studied English formally — is this too advanced for me?

No — this course is built specifically for people starting from zero, with no assumed prior knowledge.

Will I be grouped with more advanced students?

No, beginner groups are kept separate so pacing stays appropriate for true beginners.

How long until I can hold a basic conversation?

Most beginner students can manage simple everyday conversations within 6 to 8 weeks of consistent attendance.

Is explanation available in a mix of English and Urdu?

Yes — instructors use a mix of English and Urdu where helpful, especially in the earliest sessions.

Will I be embarrassed if I make mistakes?

No — this group is specifically for true beginners, so everyone is working through the same early-stage mistakes together.

How is progress measured for beginners?

Your instructor tracks specific, simple milestones — basic conversation ability, common vocabulary, pronunciation clarity — so progress is visible even early on.
