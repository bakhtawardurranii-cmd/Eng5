Questions

What Should You Know Before You Start?

Do you review writing I already have, or only assign new tasks?

Both — bring existing work like emails, essays, or IELTS practice tasks, or work through new assignments with your tutor.

How is this different from your IELTS Preparation program?

This program focuses specifically on writing; IELTS Preparation covers all four modules including Writing as one part.

Can you help with a specific document I need for work or university?

Yes — bring the specific document and we'll work on it directly alongside broader writing skills.

How is feedback delivered?

As written, line-by-line markup plus a short discussion of the recurring patterns to focus on next.

What if I don't have a specific document to work on?

That's fine — your tutor can assign relevant writing tasks based on your goals, whether that's email writing, essays, or IELTS tasks.

How quickly will I see improvement?

Most students notice fewer repeated mistakes within 3–4 sessions, since feedback is specific to their own recurring patterns.
