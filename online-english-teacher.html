Why this program

What Makes This Program Different?

Online tutoring platforms frequently rotate you between different teachers, which resets your progress every few weeks. Here, you keep the same dedicated teacher for the full duration of your program, so feedback actually compounds.

Questions

What Should You Know Before You Start?

Will I really have the same teacher every session?

Yes — you're matched with one dedicated teacher for the full duration of your program.

What if the teacher and I aren't a good fit?

Let us know and we'll arrange a switch — but this is rare given the initial matching process.

How is this different from your online speaking classes?

This is one-on-one and continuity-focused, while our speaking classes run in small live groups.

Can sessions cover both speaking and writing?

Yes, your teacher can flex session focus between speaking and writing based on your current priorities.

What if my schedule changes week to week?

Your dedicated teacher will work with you to find consistent slots, and reschedule individual sessions when needed.

Can I change focus from speaking to writing mid-program?

Yes — since it's the same teacher throughout, session focus can shift based on what you need most that week.
