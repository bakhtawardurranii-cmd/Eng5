Questions

What Should You Know Before You Start?

How is a private tutor different from your group classes?

Sessions are one-on-one and shaped entirely around your goals and pace, rather than a shared group syllabus.

How often should I book sessions?

Most students start with two sessions a week; we'll recommend a frequency based on your timeline and goals.

Can sessions be scheduled around irregular work hours?

Yes — private tutoring offers the most scheduling flexibility of any of our programs, including evenings and weekends.

Do you offer trial sessions before committing?

Yes, every new student starts with a short assessment session before we recommend a plan.

What happens in the first session?

The first session is a diagnostic assessment — your tutor listens to you speak and reviews a short writing sample to build your actual improvement plan, not a generic one.

Can I request a specific tutor?

Yes, and if a tutor isn't the right fit after your first couple of sessions, we'll help you switch at no penalty.
