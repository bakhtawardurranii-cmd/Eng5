How we compare

Should You Use an App, a Group Institute, or a Studio?

All three can teach you something. Here's the honest trade-off between them.

What matters to youSelf-study appsTypical group institutesEnglish Learning Studio
Personal, live correctionNo — automated onlyRarely — shared across a large groupYes, every session
Scored against real IELTS band descriptorsNoSometimesYes — Writing & Speaking scored to official criteria
Practice with native English speakersNoRarelyYes, built into most programs
Pace matches your actual levelFixed for everyoneFixed to the group's paceAdjusted to you from session one
Instructor credentials verifiedN/AVaries widelyCELTA, TESOL & IELTS certified
CostLowestLow–moderateHigher, reflects 1:1 attention

Apps are a reasonable starting point if you're just building vocabulary. Group institutes work if budget is the main constraint and you don't mind a shared pace. If your goal has a real deadline — a visa, an exam, a job — personal correction against real standards is what actually closes the gap.

Have Questions About Our Programs?

How do I know which program is right for me?

Use the goal-matching table above, or book a free assessment — we'll recommend the right program based on a short conversation and writing sample, not a sales pitch.

Can I switch programs after starting?

Yes. If your goals change or we find a better fit after your first few sessions, you can move between programs without starting over.

Do you offer a free trial or consultation?

Yes — every new student starts with a short assessment call so we understand your current level and goals before recommending a program.

Are classes available online and in-person?

Both. Most programs are available either in person at our Karachi location or live online, and you can choose per session in many cases.
