IELTS Band 6 Requirements: What Each Module Needs

Band 6 — Competent User — is a common requirement for university admission. Here's exactly what it takes in each of the four IELTS modules.

What Does Band 6 Require in Each Module?

Listening

Roughly 23/40 correct answers, with solid performance on the more straightforward Sections 1 and 2.

Reading

Around 23/40 correct in Academic, with reasonable pacing across all 3 passages.

Writing

Task addressed overall, adequate vocabulary and grammar range, with some inaccuracies that don't block meaning.

Speaking

Willingness to speak at length with some hesitation, a workable range of vocabulary and structures.

Where Band 6 sits

Is Band 6 Enough for You?

Band 6 — "Competent User" — is enough for many university programs and some visa categories, but several skilled-migration and professional-registration routes specifically require Band 7. If you're not sure which band your target actually needs, check the official requirements for your specific visa or program first, then compare that to our Band 7 requirements guide to see the gap.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many correct answers do I need for Band 6 in Reading or Listening?

Typically around 23 out of 40 correct in Academic Reading, and similarly around 23 out of 40 in Listening — though this shifts slightly between test versions. General Training Reading usually needs a few more correct answers for the same band.

What does Band 6 Writing look like?

A Band 6 response addresses the task, though some parts may be underdeveloped, uses adequate vocabulary with some inaccuracies, and mixes simple and complex sentences with some grammar errors that don't seriously impede communication.

What does Band 6 Speaking sound like?

Willingness to speak at length, though with some hesitation, a mix of simple and complex language with mixed accuracy, and pronunciation that's generally clear despite some mispronunciations.

Is Band 6 considered a good score?

Band 6 is classified as 'Competent User' — someone with generally effective command of the language despite some inaccuracies. It's sufficient for many university programs and some visa categories, though several skilled-migration routes require Band 7 or higher.

How long does it usually take to move from Band 5 to Band 6?

This varies by starting level and how much time you can dedicate, but most students see it as a shorter jump than 6 to 7, often achievable in 4–8 weeks of focused practice.
