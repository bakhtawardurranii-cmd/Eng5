Grammar Quiz

10 questions covering the grammar mistakes we see most often — including a few straight from our "Margin Corrections." Answer all of them to see your score with explanations.

1. She ___ in Karachi since 2015.

2. He don't like coffee.

3. I am agree with your plan.

4. We have been waiting ___ two hours.

5. Please discuss ___ the proposal in the meeting.

6. If I ___ more time, I would have finished it.

7. Kindly revert back to me by Friday.

8. She is married ___ a doctor.

9. Neither of the answers ___ correct.

10. I look forward to ___ from you.
