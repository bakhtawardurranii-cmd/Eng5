English Level Test

10 questions across grammar and sentence structure, ranging from beginner to advanced. Answer all of them, then see your estimated CEFR level (A1–C2) instantly.

2. I ___ my homework before dinner yesterday.

3. If it rains tomorrow, we ___ the picnic.

4. By the time you arrive, I ___ dinner.

5. Choose the correct sentence.

6. The report ___ by the team before the deadline.

7. Which word best fits: "The negotiations reached a ___ after months of talks."
