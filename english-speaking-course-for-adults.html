What Makes This Program Different?

Most speaking courses in Karachi are timed and paced for students, not working adults. This one runs on evening and weekend slots, with scenarios built around meetings, interviews, and workplace English — not classroom small talk.

Questions

What Should You Know Before You Start?

What times are classes available?

Evening and weekend slots are available specifically for working professionals, alongside standard daytime timing.

Is this different from your beginner course?

Yes — this program assumes some existing English and focuses on professional, workplace-relevant scenarios rather than starting from zero.

Can my company enroll multiple employees?

Yes, we offer group arrangements for companies — get in touch to discuss your team's needs.

Do sessions focus on my specific industry?

Where possible, we tailor scenarios and vocabulary to your field during one-on-one or small-group sessions.

Can this fit around a full-time job?

Yes — that's specifically what this program is built for, with evening and weekend session options.

Is this suitable if I'm already fairly fluent?

Yes, sessions adjust to your starting level, including polishing an already-solid base for higher-stakes professional situations.
