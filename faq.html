FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about our programs, the free IELTS mock test, and how sessions work. Can't find what you need? Get in touch directly.

General

What is English Learning Studio?

A premium English and IELTS coaching studio in Karachi, offering both in-person and online programs for adults — professionals, students, jobseekers, and IELTS candidates.

Do you teach online, in person, or both?

Both. Most programs are available either at our Karachi location or live online, and many students mix the two depending on their week.

Do I need to already know some English to join?

No — we have a dedicated program for true beginners, as well as programs for intermediate and advanced learners.

How do I know which program is right for me?

Use the goal-matching table on our Programs page, or book a free assessment call and we'll recommend a program based on a short conversation and writing sample.

Programs & Enrollment

How long do programs typically take?

It depends on the program and your starting level — most run 6 to 12 weeks, though private tutoring can be shorter or longer depending on your goal.

Can I switch programs after starting?

Yes. If your goals change, you can move between programs without starting over.

Do you offer a free trial or consultation?

Yes — every new student starts with a short, free assessment call before committing to a program.

Can companies enroll multiple employees for Business English?

Yes, we run group arrangements for companies. Contact us to discuss team size and scheduling.

Free IELTS Mock Test

Is the free IELTS mock test really free?

Yes, all four modules are assessed at no cost, with no obligation to enroll afterward.

Is the mock test an official IELTS score?

No — it's an independent assessment scored using official band descriptors, not administered by IDP or the British Council.

Payments & Policies

What payment methods do you accept?

Details to follow — contact us directly and we'll confirm current payment options.

What is your rescheduling or cancellation policy?

We understand schedules change — reach out as early as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate a reschedule.

Still have a question?

Reach out directly and we'll get back to you within one working day.
